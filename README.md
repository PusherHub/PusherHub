### SDKs

| Platform | Repository |
|---|---|
| Flutter (Android + iOS) | [pusherhub-flutter](https://github.com/PusherHub/pusherhub-flutter) |
| Android — Kotlin and Java | [pusherhub-android](https://github.com/PusherHub/pusherhub-android) |
| iOS — Swift | [pusherhub-ios](https://github.com/PusherHub/pusherhub-ios) |
| React Native (Android + iOS) | [pusherhub-react-native](https://github.com/PusherHub/pusherhub-react-native) |
| Unity (Android + iOS) | [pusherhub-unity](https://github.com/PusherHub/pusherhub-unity) |

Each SDK is a single file you add to your app. Your app's **SDK Integration** tab in PusherHub shows every step with your keys filled in.

### What you can do

- **Push notifications** — to everyone, a segment, or one signed-in user; now, scheduled, or recurring
- **In-app messages** — banners, modals, full screens, popups and bottom sheets, shown when people open your app
- **Your own Firebase project** — notifications come from your app, and your audience data stays yours
- **Analytics** — delivery, opens and clicks; devices by country, platform and app version
- **REST API and webhooks** — send from your own server, and get told when things happen

### Get started

1. Create a free account at [pusherhub.com](https://pusherhub.com) and add your app.
2. Connect your Firebase project.
3. Add the SDK for your platform, and send your first notification.

Follow the [step-by-step guides](https://pusherhub.com/docs).
